# FELYA Negative Logo Optical Compensation

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-25

## Scope

This decision applies to the FELYA image mark inside the white negative
wordmark used by the website. The black positive logo remains the
authoritative, unchanged designer geometry. The `FELYA` wordmark path is
geometrically identical in both versions.

## Problem

Mathematically identical black and white shapes do not appear optically equal.
Because of the positive-negative, or irradiation, effect, the white mark on a
dark background appears heavier and its open inner negative channel appears
narrower.

The preceding forensic validation established identical source geometry and
identical browser bounding boxes for the former black and white assets. After
normalization it measured IoU `1.0`, XOR `0`, and an identical negative space.
It also excluded CSS, DOM, cache, browser rasterization, and subpixel position
as relevant causes. The difference is perceptual, not a browser defect.

## Decision

N025 is the official website negative correction. Only the white image mark is
eroded inward; the white wordmark remains unchanged. The SVG is generated by
`assets-source/brand/generate-negative-logo-optical.mjs` from the protected
V2-derived website geometry. The operation is a deterministic SVG vector-mask
erosion with round joins and caps; it does not use raster tracing or a CSS
workaround.

Parameters:

- Designer master: `felya-logo-zwischenstand-2026-09-25_bildmarke-v2.svg`
- Designer master SHA256: `4c206658287d06db901e058a83ee3de31f19b7943774adeaf6341aa974c0b203`
- Website image-mark path SHA256: `a1ff0a90c54f06fee821b3d281d51083b88fbdde97907a8ab0859d8c94c292c2`
- Reference render height: `28.443003 CSS px`
- Effective compensation: `0.25 CSS px`
- Source offset: `3.836317`
- White-area change from N0: approximately `-9.21%`
- Measured open inner-channel area change from N0: approximately `+7.39%`

N0 retained the optically heavy white appearance. N025 provides a subtle
correction without making the mark appear to be a different construction.
N050 changed the weight more strongly. N075 visibly changed the rasterized
bounding box. N025 was therefore selected as the balance between fidelity and
optical equivalence.

## Application

- Light website theme: unchanged black positive logo.
- Dark website theme: N025 white negative logo.
- Header and footer use the same central theme-aware asset configuration.
- The public social preview is a black positive application on a light
  background and remains unchanged.
- The favicon family remains a black positive mark on a white continuous-corner
  surface and receives no N025 compensation.

The negative version is a deliberate optical brand variant, not a technical
browser workaround.

## Designer handoff

N025 is the accepted optical negative version for the website. For the final
brand handoff, this decision should be reviewed with the designer and, where
appropriate, added as the official negative master to the FELYA Brand Guide.
Until then, the website may use the technically documented and reproducible
N025 asset.
